Saving an AI chat shouldn't be this hard.

The built-in options are a full-account data dump that arrives by email, a public share link, or copy-paste that wrecks your formatting. And the extensions that fill the gap have their own problems:

They break

Chat sites change their layout constantly. Exporters that depend on one fragile hook stop working — often right when you need them.

They paywall the basics

Daily download caps or a subscription just to save your own conversation once.

They want your data

Some tools need an account or send conversations to a server to "process" them.

FAQ

Do my chats get uploaded anywhere?

No. ConvoBinder reads the conversation you have open, only when you click Export, and builds the file locally. There are no ConvoBinder servers and no analytics. The only network request is Pro license activation/validation with Lemon Squeezy.

Do I need an account?

No. Install and export. Pro uses a license key you paste into the popup — no login.

Which sites are supported?

chatgpt.com (and chat.openai.com), claude.ai and gemini.google.com. Tell me which site you'd like next.

What's actually free?

Exporting the open chat to Markdown and plain text, unlimited, forever. Pro adds PDF, HTML, JSON and choosing specific messages.

How does PDF export work?

Pro opens a clean, print-ready page and your browser's print dialog — choose "Save as PDF". Text stays selectable and searchable.

Very long chats?

ConvoBinder exports the messages loaded on the page. On some sites very long chats load older messages lazily — scroll to the top first so everything is loaded, then export.

What happens when ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini change their layout?

ConvoBinder tries several detection methods per site, so small changes often don't break it. If an export comes out wrong, email SUPPORT_EMAIL with the site and what you saw, and I'll prioritise a fix.

Is $29 a subscription?

No. One payment, all future updates, up to 3 browsers. 30-day money-back guarantee if it doesn't work for you.

Why these permissions?

Access to the three chat sites is needed to read the conversation you choose to export; storage remembers your license; api.lemonsqueezy.com validates Pro keys. Nothing else.
