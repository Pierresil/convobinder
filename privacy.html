ChatKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

ChatKeep ("the extension") exports AI chat conversations you are viewing to files on your computer.

Data we collect: None. We do not collect, store, sell or transmit your conversations, personal information, or browsing history.

How it works: When you click Export, the extension reads the conversation text on the open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini tab, converts it in your browser, and saves a file to your device. Nothing is sent to our servers (we have none for this purpose).

Storage: The extension uses browser local storage only to remember your Pro license key and activation status.

Network requests: The only network requests are to the Lemon Squeezy license API (api.lemonsqueezy.com), made when you activate, validate or deactivate a Pro license. Only your license key and an anonymous instance name are sent.

Permissions: "storage" saves license status. Host access to chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com is needed to read the open conversation. Host access to api.lemonsqueezy.com is for license checks.

Third parties: Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which has its own privacy policy. We do not share data with anyone else.

Children: Not directed to children under 13.

Changes: Updates will be posted here with a new date.
